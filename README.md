# Alfido Tech Customer Transaction & Behavior Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer transactions and purchasing behavior to identify customer segments, purchase patterns, retention trends, and churn risks.

The analysis was performed using Python and RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) customer segmentation techniques.

The insights generated from this analysis can help Alfido Tech improve customer engagement, reduce customer churn, and increase customer lifetime value.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objectives of this project are:

- Perform data cleaning and preprocessing
- Create meaningful customer-level features
- Segment customers using RFM analysis
- Profile each customer segment
- Analyze customer purchase patterns
- Visualize revenue and retention trends
- Identify customers with potential churn risk
- Provide actionable business recommendations

## ⚙️ 2. Feature Engineering

Customer-level features were created from transaction data.

### RFM Analysis

RFM analysis was used to understand customer purchasing behavior.

### Recency

Recency measures how recently a customer made a purchase.

```text
Lower Recency = More Recent Purchase
```

Customers with recent purchases are generally more engaged.

---

### Frequency

Frequency measures the total number of purchases made by a customer.

```text
Higher Frequency = More Engaged Customer
```

Customers who purchase more frequently are more likely to become loyal customers.

---

### Monetary

Monetary measures the total amount spent by a customer.

```text
Higher Monetary Value = Higher Customer Value
```

Customers with higher monetary value contribute more to business revenue.

---

## 👥 3. Customer Segmentation

Customers were segmented based on their RFM scores.

The following customer segments were identified:

---

### 🏆 Champions

Champions are customers who purchased recently, frequently, and spent a high amount.

#### Recommended Strategy

* VIP loyalty programs
* Exclusive discounts
* Early access to new products
* Referral rewards
* Personalized recommendations

---

### 💎 Loyal / High Potential

These customers show strong engagement and have the potential to become high-value customers.

#### Recommended Strategy

* Cross-selling
* Product recommendations
* Loyalty rewards
* Personalized promotions
* Bundle offers

---

### 🆕 Recent / New

These customers have recently made purchases but have relatively lower purchase frequency.

#### Recommended Strategy

* Welcome campaigns
* Second-purchase offers
* Product recommendations
* Follow-up messages
* Limited-time discounts

---

### ⚠️ At Risk – High Value

These customers have previously generated high value but have not purchased recently.

#### Recommended Strategy

* Win-back campaigns
* Personalized discounts
* Reminder notifications
* Special offers based on previous purchases
* Personalized email campaigns

---

### 💤 Hibernating

These customers have been inactive for a long period.

#### Recommended Strategy

* Re-engagement campaigns
* Special comeback offers
* Low-cost marketing campaigns
* Personalized reminders

---

### 🔔 Needs Attention

These customers have moderate engagement and require targeted strategies to improve their activity.

#### Recommended Strategy

* Personalized promotions
* Product recommendations
* Cross-selling
* Engagement campaigns

---

## 📊 4. Purchase Pattern Analysis

Purchase patterns were analyzed to understand customer buying behavior.

The analysis includes:

* Monthly revenue trends
* Number of orders
* Number of active customers
* Product category performance
* Average order value

### Key Analysis

Monthly revenue trends help identify changes in customer purchasing activity over time.

Product category analysis helps identify:

* High-performing product categories
* Categories generating maximum revenue
* Categories with higher customer demand
* Opportunities for cross-selling and promotional campaigns

---

## 🔁 5. Customer Retention Analysis

Customer retention was analyzed by studying repeat purchase behavior.

The following metrics were analyzed:

* Active customers
* Repeat customers
* Repeat customer rate
* Retention trends over time

### Repeat Customer Rate

The repeat customer rate helps measure the percentage of customers who make more than one purchase.

A higher repeat customer rate indicates stronger customer engagement and customer retention.

---

## ⚠️ 6. Churn Risk Analysis

Churn risk analysis was performed to identify customers who may stop purchasing from the business.

Customers were categorized based on their purchase recency.

### Churn Risk Categories

| Risk Level  | Description                                            |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Low Risk    | Customers with recent purchase activity                |
| Medium Risk | Customers who have not purchased for a moderate period |
| High Risk   | Customers who have not purchased for a long period     |

This analysis helps businesses prioritize customer retention efforts.

---

## 📈 7. Business Insights

The analysis provides the following business insights:

* High-value customers should be prioritized for retention.
* Recent customers require strategies to encourage their second purchase.
* Inactive customers require re-engagement campaigns.
* Champions should be rewarded to maintain long-term loyalty.
* Customer purchase history can be used for personalized marketing.
* RFM segmentation helps businesses target different customer groups effectively.
* Retention campaigns can be prioritized based on customer inactivity and churn risk.

---

## 💡 8. Actionable Recommendations

### 1. Launch High-Value Win-Back Campaigns

Target At Risk – High Value customers with personalized offers and product recommendations based on their previous purchase history.

This can help recover valuable customers before they completely churn.

---

### 2. Improve Second-Purchase Conversion

Target Recent / New customers with:

* Post-purchase follow-ups
* Complementary product recommendations
* Limited-time offers
* Personalized discounts

The goal is to convert first-time buyers into repeat customers.

---

### 3. Introduce a VIP Loyalty Program

Reward Champions with:

* Loyalty points
* Exclusive discounts
* Early access to new products
* Referral benefits
* Personalized offers

This can help retain the most valuable customers.

---

### 4. Use Automated Lifecycle Retention Campaigns

Create automated campaigns based on customer inactivity.

Example:

| Inactivity Period | Campaign                 |
| ----------------- | ------------------------ |
| 90 Days           | Reminder Campaign        |
| 180 Days          | Personalized Offer       |
| 365+ Days         | Strong Win-Back Campaign |

This helps Alfido Tech proactively engage customers before they churn.

---

### 5. Use Category-Based Personalization

Use customer purchase history to provide:

* Relevant product recommendations
* Category-specific promotions
* Personalized product bundles
* Cross-selling opportunities

Personalized marketing can be more effective than sending generic promotions to all customers.
